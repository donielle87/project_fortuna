About the Wisconsin Lottery

  • Where the Money Goes
  • History of WI Lottery
  • Financials

Since 1988, the Wisconsin Lottery has managed the Lottery fund with the following results*:

Over $12.0 Billion Prizes Paid

(57.0% of revenue)

At least 50% of total revenue must be dedicated to prize winnings.

Over $6.4 Billion Funding for Property Tax Credits

(30.5% of revenue)

This money has been returned to eligible Wisconsin taxpayers.

Over $1.3 Billion Retailer Compensation Paid

(6.4% of revenue)

This money goes to Wisconsin businesses.

Over $1.2 Billion Operational Expenses

(6.1% of revenue)

Includes expenses such as salaries, employee benefits, supplies and services. The Lottery has operated well below the mandated limit of 10%.

Since 1988, the Wisconsin Lottery has generated more than $21.1 Billion in total revenue. Over 93% of that revenue has gone back to winners, retailers, and Wisconsin homeowners.

 

*Figures are from inception through Fiscal Year (FY) 2025. Amounts from FY2025 are preliminary as of December 2025, and subject to change until completion of the audit. Percentages may not sum to 100% due to rounding.
